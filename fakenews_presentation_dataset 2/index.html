How superficial 97% training accuracy masked critical vulnerability to keyword bias

📊 The 97% Accuracy Illusion

Standard NLP pipelines (TF-IDF + Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, simple SpaCy textcats):

COVID Model Accuracy: 96.99%
Macro F1 Score: 0.9533
Afghanistan Accuracy: 83.33%

The Fatal Flaw: Error & confidence analysis revealed the model was not reading meaning—it simply correlated sensational words like 'Trump', 'coronavirus', and 'Gates' with FAKE labels.

! Empirical False Alarm Evidence

"Says of President Donald Trump's actions on coronavirus: No. 1, he fired the pandemic team... No. 2, he's been defunding CDC."
Actual: REAL • Predicted: FAKE • Confidence: 94.36%!

Core Realization: Truth is an external factual relation, NOT an intrinsic property of sentence grammar. A model cannot verify news without cross-referencing verified knowledge.

Semantic Passage Retrieval & Sliding Chunk Flowchart

Granular context chunking and dense vector indexing with hardware acceleration

Chunking Rationale

Why not whole documents? Embedding an entire 3,000-word research paper averages and dilutes specific factual claims.

Why 25-word overlap? Factual refutations (e.g. "However, clinical trials showed no efficacy...") often span across chunk boundaries. Overlap ensures zero sentence loss.

Logical Verification

Cross-Encoder Natural Language Inference (DeBERTa-v3)

Why joint full-attention reasoning is fundamentally superior to vector similarity

≠ Bi-Encoder Limitation (Topical Only)

Consider two opposite claims about medicine:

Claim A: "Antibiotics kill bacterial infections."
Claim B: "Antibiotics kill viral infections."

A Bi-Encoder vector embedding produces 0.91 Cosine Similarity between both claims because they share nearly identical vocabulary.

Conclusion: Bi-encoders know what text is about, but cannot determine whether one statement logically contradicts another.

🧠 Cross-Encoder DeBERTa-v3 Reasoner

Feeds Evidence and Claim simultaneously into cross-attention layers:

[CLS] Evidence Text [SEP] Target Claim [SEP]

Every token in the claim actively computes cross-attention weights against every word in the evidence:

  • Entailment: Evidence proves claim ($P \ge 0.70$)
  • Contradiction: Evidence refutes claim ($P \ge 0.70$)
  • Neutral: Related but unprovable

Responsible AI

System Limitations, Guardrails & Ethical AI

Ensuring transparent decision-support rather than unchecked automated censorship

Technical Limitations

1. NLI Scores are NOT Truth Values:
DeBERTa evaluates logical relations between two text strings. If the underlying evidence document contains biased or outdated data, the conclusion reflects that bias.

2. Knowledge Base Horizon:
Veritas is only as knowledgeable as its verified evidence repository. Uncataloged claims trigger "Insufficient Evidence".

3. Cross-Encoder Latency:
Joint token attention is compute-intensive, requiring strict Top-K pruning ($K \le 5$).

Ethical Principles & Guardrails

Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support:
Veritas is architected to empower fact-checkers, journalists, and citizens, NOT to act as an opaque automated censorship mechanism.

Mandatory Provenance & Citations:
No claim is ever labeled without rendering the originating publisher, HTTPS link, and exact excerpt.

Conservative Uncertainty Handling:
Veritas defaults to "Insufficient Evidence" or "Inconclusive" rather than hallucinating false verdicts.
